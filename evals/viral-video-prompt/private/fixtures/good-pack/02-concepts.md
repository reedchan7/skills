## Concept A — capacity reveal  ·  the convention bet

- **Target metric**: views/completion — resolves, last frame matches the first.
- **Audience segment**: office workers 28-45, the largest end-use segment; needs to see that it does not read as a lunch box on a desk.
- **The proof**: capacity → the stack that should not fit, fitting → a 13-inch laptop as the comparison object. Renders reliably? yes.
- **Frame-one claim**: the closed bag stands shorter than the stack of containers leaning against it.
- **Generic-swap test**: **pass** — a slouchy rival cannot stand square while a stack leans on it.
- **Competitor-frame test**: **pass** — the empty bag holding its own square with nothing supporting it · in frame one: **yes**
- **Glance test**: **pass** — two objects touching, one visibly shorter than the other.
- **Hook restatement**: In the first frame I see a short navy box beside a taller stack; within three seconds the stack starts going in; that makes me want to know whether the lid closes; it points at the product's squared interior.
- **On a fail**: no test failed.
- **Change density**: 8 changes · largest gap 2 s · 1 cuts · hero moment is the loaded bag standing closed.

## Concept B — price shock  ·  the transplant bet

- **Target metric**: comments — ends on a question about the viewer's commute.
- **Audience segment**: healthcare and shift workers 25-50, who weigh function over aesthetics.
- **The proof**: structure → it holds its square while the others slump → the two rivals as comparison objects. Renders reliably? yes.
- **Frame-one claim**: the bag sits on a shelf between two visibly cheaper bags of the same size.
- **Generic-swap test**: **pass** — the sentence carries this product's price tier and its stand-up walls.
- **Competitor-frame test**: **pass** — three bags side by side where only one holds its shape · in frame one: **yes**
- **Glance test**: **pass** — three same-size objects, one of them square and two of them slumped.
- **Hook restatement**: In the first frame I see three bags of one size where only one stands up; within three seconds a hand presses each in turn; that makes me want to know which one costs least; it points at the product's structure.
- **On a fail**: no test failed.
- **Change density**: 7 changes · largest gap 3 s · 0 cuts · hero moment is the one bag still square.
