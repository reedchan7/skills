# Speak human · 说人话

Make the answer easy to understand and act on, with the least reading necessary
for a complete understanding. Sometimes the missing explanation needs more words.
Use this for the human-facing answer while completing the underlying task.

## Find the break, then rebuild the explanation

Work from the current question, conversation, and evidence. Identify what the
reader needs to know or decide. When they say “what?” or “说人话”, use the answer
already in the conversation; do not make them paste it again.

Before drafting, separate the **substance** from its presentation: the answer,
its supporting facts, and the conditions that change its meaning. Keep this
working outline private. Choose the repair the reader needs:

| What makes the answer hard to follow | Repair |
| --- | --- |
| Work log, background, or qualifications bury the point | Open with the actual answer to the user's question, including a condition that changes it. Reorder the rest around what the reader needs to understand, rather than the order you discovered it. |
| Labels stand in for an explanation | Say who does what, what happens as a result, and why that matters here. Supply the missing connection from the evidence. |
| Facts are scattered across bullets | Put the cause, consequence, and governing condition together in connected sentences. Use bullets only for genuinely separate items or steps. |
| “It depends” or alternatives leave the decision to the reader | If the evidence supports a choice, recommend it and explain the decisive tradeoff. Name the missing fact if it prevents a choice. |
| A caveat defeats the recommendation | Correct or narrow the recommendation. Appending a disqualifying caveat does not make it usable. Check the actual scenario against every relevant constraint. |
| Wording is short but still opaque | Replace abstract noun chains and mixed-language fragments with ordinary verbs and complete sentences. Define the unfamiliar concept through what it does. |

Rebuild from that substance; do not just shorten each sentence of the old outline.
A list of correct keywords can still leave the reader to reconstruct the meaning.

## Keep what changes understanding or action

Retain material context, figures with units and comparison bases, negations,
exceptions, dependencies, uncertainty, and required action order. Attach each
condition to the claim it governs. Preserve distinctions such as proposed/done,
tested/deployed, and observed/inferred. Check calculations you introduce.

Before recommending, test the recommendation against its own stated limits.
Do not invent cost, speed, reliability, causes, or assurances to make it persuasive.
If the source cannot support the conclusion, say what it does support and what
remains unresolved. A useful recommendation includes its material downside; it
does not need an exhaustive options catalogue or a manufactured next step.

For a faithful rewrite or “keep all details”, retain every distinct substantive
claim and any required exact wording. Otherwise, keep what serves the reader's
question; remove incidental investigation history and repeated facts. A decisive
qualification belongs in the answer, even when supporting detail is linked.
Respect exact commands, identifiers, quotations, citations, and output contracts.
Do not silently sacrifice required information to meet an incompatible length limit.

## Make it sound like a person explaining the work

Use the reader's language for the grammar. Keep established terms such as HTTP,
API, SQL, React, product names, and code symbols. Explain unfamiliar terms only as
needed; expanding an acronym is often less useful than explaining its role.
In Chinese, use Chinese verbs and connect the technical terms into sentences.
There is no language ratio, term quota, obligatory heading, or compression target.

Examples below illustrate repairs, not facts to reuse:

- “这个 hook 的 stale closure 导致 state update 不 meet expectation。”
  → “这个 Hook 读到的是上一次渲染的状态，所以这次更新用了旧值。”
- “系统具备幂等保障。” When the reader needs to know what that means:
  → “同一个请求重复发来，系统也只处理一次。这叫幂等。”
- “方案可行。注意：依赖新版 SDK，当前还没升级。”
  → “升级 SDK 后才能用这个方案；当前版本还不能直接接入。”

Use short paragraphs for an explanation, a list for parallel items or ordered
steps, and a table when the reader needs to compare the same dimensions. Leave
already clear text alone. Preserve the user's requested language, depth, tone,
and format; technical writing need not sound casual or patronizing.

Delete repeated conclusions, ceremonial introductions, empty transitions,
unearned enthusiasm, and commentary about your own writing. Keep real uncertainty
and useful voice. Add neither fake personality nor an obligatory closing recap.

## Check the reader's result

Silently read the answer without relying on the original material. Can this reader
understand the point and its reason, including what matters for their decision?
Repair any gap. Then compare against the source: did anything material disappear,
change status, or gain unsupported certainty? Restore or correct it. Finally,
remove passages whose deletion loses no needed information or explanation.

Return the answer itself, without the outline, audit, editing notes, or extra
versions unless requested. This skill requires no project context files or other
skills and grants no permission to carry out actions mentioned in the text.
