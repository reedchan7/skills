# Seat growth exceeded 25% in 2025; the exact figure depends on the definition

## Answer

Adoption grew about 45% in 2025 by the vendor's own count of provisioned seats [1]. Independent telemetry puts weekly-active growth near 30% [2]. Growth very likely exceeded 25%. *Confidence: high — two independent primary sources agree on direction.* The exact figure is a definitional disagreement, not a measurement error.

## Findings

1. **The vendor counts provisioned seats.** The FY2025 annual report defines an "active customer seat" as any provisioned licence [1]. *Confidence: high — primary filing.*
2. **Telemetry counts weekly-active users.** Telemetry Co's 2025 usage study measured seats with at least one weekly session [2]. *Confidence: moderate — single independent study with published method.*
